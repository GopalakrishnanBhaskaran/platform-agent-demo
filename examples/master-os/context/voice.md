# My email voice

Used by the `email-reply` skill. The `email-coach` skill proposes changes to this file. I approve them before they land.

## Rules

- Answer first. The first line says what they need to know or do.
- Short: under 120 words unless I ask for more.
- Steps as a numbered list, one action per step.
- One clear ask at the end, with a date if there's a deadline.
- Plain words. No "I hope this finds you well", no "please don't hesitate to reach out".
- Ticket replies: include the ticket ID in the subject and say what was changed.

## Sign-off

- Org: "Thanks, <name> | Platform Team"
- Personal and clients: "Thanks, <name>"

## Examples (real sent emails, edit these)

> Hi Priya, your job failed because the cluster policy blocks `Standard_E64s_v5`. Fix:
> 1. Edit the job cluster and pick `Standard_E16s_v5`.
> 2. Re-run.
> If you need the bigger node, reply with the business reason and I'll raise an exception.
